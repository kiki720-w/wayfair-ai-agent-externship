Executive Summary

Wayfair's sampled assortment sits above Amazon and Walmart on price. The strongest near-term opportunity is a focused washable-rug proposition combining better discovery, size and care confidence, and a curated $80–$150 test assortment—not blanket price matching.

  • Washable language is a visible competitive battleground.
  • Amazon and Walmart show stronger sub-$100 representation in the sample.
  • Wayfair should protect differentiation through curation and trust while testing accessibility.

Walmart

Cross-competitor signal: care convenience and price accessibility should be monitored together with assortment clarity.

Strategic Recommendations

  1. Launch a focused assortment test: select 20–30 washable rugs in the $80–$150 band and compare conversion, margin, and returns with controls.
  2. Strengthen discovery: add washable and backing filters, size visualization, care verification, and room-fit content.
  3. Build a weekly market pulse: store price-band and review deltas and alert only on material changes.
  4. Create a supplier scorecard: assess MOQ, landed cost, lead time, wash testing, compliance, and defect rate.

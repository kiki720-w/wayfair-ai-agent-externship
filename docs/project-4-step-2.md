# Project 4 Step 2 — Enhancement Plan

## Enhancement direction

The selected direction combines all three proposed improvement areas:

- **A — Brand voice:** make the output warm, specific, conversational, practical, and recognizably Wayfair while blocking generic AI clichés.
- **B — New content types:** add full email sequences, short-form video scripts, paid-ad variants, product copy, KPIs, and quick wins.
- **C — Expanded inputs:** add target audience, season, campaign objective, product focus, and competitor focus.

## Node-level plan

- **Upload Form:** add the new creative-brief fields with examples and defaults.
- **Extract Files:** normalize the new form context alongside the Project 2 and Project 3 evidence.
- **AI Content:** add the Wayfair persona, tone and grounding rules, new deliverables, and an expanded fixed JSON schema.
- **Parse Content:** validate required keys and provide readable failure handling.
- **Build HTML:** display the input brief, channel cards, quick wins, KPIs, and evaluation summary.
- **Download / Preview:** preserve one-click HTML delivery and browser preview.
- **Workflow-level evaluation:** compare three representative briefs against the original agent on brand fit, specificity, grounding, usefulness, and structural validity.

## Success criteria

- At least 80% of brand-voice criteria score 4/5 or higher, with no banned AI clichés.
- Audience, season, and objective changes produce meaningfully different strategy and copy.
- All required old and new content sections appear.
- Three of three test runs return valid parseable JSON with no missing required fields.
- Recommendations remain grounded in the uploaded reports.
- HTML preview and download complete successfully after every tested run.
